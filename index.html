Privacy Policy

Welcome to ImagoFind. This Privacy Policy explains how the app accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use its photo search and text recognition features. We aim to keep your personal photos and information private.

1. Introduction

ImagoFind is an Android application designed to help users search and organize photos using image recognition and Optical Character Recognition (OCR).

The app is designed to process gallery data locally wherever possible.

2. Information We Access

Photos and Images

ImagoFind requests permission to access photos on your device so you can search and browse your gallery.

Images may be used to:

  • Display your photo gallery.
  • Generate searchable information.
  • Identify visible objects and scenes.
  • Extract text from images using OCR.
  • Help you find photos based on search queries.

Photos are not uploaded to our servers as part of the app's local gallery indexing functionality.

Extracted Text and Search Index

ImagoFind may extract text from images using OCR and create a local search index. This information is used to help you find images by searching for words or phrases.

Device Information

The app may access basic device information required for its operation, depending on your Android device and the services integrated into the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information accessed by ImagoFind is used to provide:

  • Photo search by text.
  • Image searches based on visual content.
  • Text extraction from images.
  • Local search indexing.
  • App functionality and performance improvements.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Local Data Storage

ImagoFind is designed to process and store gallery search information locally on your device.

The local search index may contain extracted text and information needed to locate your photos.

You can clear the local ImagoFind index through the app's settings. This removes the locally stored search information created by the app, not your original photos.

Uninstalling the app may remove its locally stored application data, subject to Android's storage behavior.

5. AI and Third-Party Services

Some features may use artificial intelligence or third-party services, such as Google's Gemini or Firebase AI.

If these services are enabled, certain image content, extracted text, or search information may be transmitted to the relevant service to provide the requested functionality.

The information shared depends on the feature you use and how it is implemented. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share photos or extracted text with third parties for advertising purposes as part of local indexing functionality.

Information may be processed by third-party services when you use features that depend on them, such as online AI functionality or advertising, if included in the app.

7. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in ImagoFind, the app may use third-party advertising services to display ads.

These services may collect information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interaction data, according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by ImagoFind. Locally stored search information is kept within the app's storage on your device, subject to Android's security features.

When information is transmitted to third-party services, its security and handling may also depend on the relevant service provider.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

ImagoFind does not maintain a personal account or a central database of users' gallery photos as part of its local indexing functionality.

Search indexes and related information are stored locally on your device. You can remove the local search index through the app's settings or uninstall the app to remove its application data, subject to Android's behavior.

Third-party services have their own data retention and deletion practices.

10. Children's Privacy

ImagoFind is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information for our own purposes.

If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You can control the app's access to your photos through your Android device settings.

You can also clear the local search index through ImagoFind's settings.

You may choose not to use features that require optional permissions or third-party services, although some features may not work without them.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or data practices change. Updated policies will be published at the same privacy policy URL with a revised effective date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or ImagoFind's data practices, please contact us.
